## 📊 Sector Overview & Market Dynamics 📈

The global **AI Agent & Intelligent Browser Automation** market is valued at approximately **$10.9 Billion in 2026** (part of the broader $167B+ intelligent process automation ecosystem) and is projected to expand rapidly at a **43%–46% CAGR** toward **$50+ Billion by 2030**.

The sector is currently **moderately fragmented**: foundational headless browser execution and proxy stealth infrastructure show signs of early consolidation among a few high-velocity cloud providers (e.g., Browserbase, Browserless, Steel.dev), whereas application-level web agents, vision-first Large Action Models (LAMs), and specialized in-page GUI copilots remain highly competitive and diversified with thriving open-source innovation.
